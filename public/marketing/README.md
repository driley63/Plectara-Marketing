| File | Use |
| --- | --- |

The watch artwork combines the case and screen from the supplied
Apple Watch simulator capture with a dark woven band. The detached simulator
toolbar and exterior shadow were removed. The band is illustrative; the watch
app screen and case come from the supplied capture without redrawing the UI.

App screen PNGs include the phone bezel and side buttons, with transparent space
outside the device. Widget PNGs are cropped to the rounded widget edges with
transparent exterior corners. Drop a file into this folder using the filename
above; the homepage picks it up automatically.

The yarn WebP is copied unchanged from the approved PL-OS app visual kit at
`assets/app/plectara/textures/plectara-widget-yarn-gentle-focus-v1.webp`.
The six device PNGs are exact crops of user-supplied iPhone 16 simulator
captures from September 30, 2026. `hero-phone.png` and `today-tab.png` use the
same Today capture; the other four show their matching app screens. The detached
simulator toolbar, exterior shadows, and unused transparent space were removed.
The screen and bezel pixels were not redrawn.
The medium and large widget PNGs are exact crops of user-supplied iPhone 16
simulator screenshots; no widget content was redrawn. The download section uses
a brand-color gradient until verified lifestyle photography is supplied. New
photography can be added later without changing the page layout.

PNG screenshots must retain their RGBA alpha channel, including partially
transparent pixels along curved edges. Do not flatten them or bake a
transparency checkerboard into the artwork.
